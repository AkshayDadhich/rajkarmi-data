RajKarmi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

RajKarmi ("the app") is an independent app for Rajasthan government employees. It is not affiliated with the Government of Rajasthan. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data stored only on your phone

Pay details you enter (pay level, basic pay, allowances, deductions), your holiday reminders and settings are saved on your device. They are never uploaded to us.

Data shared with services we use

  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google): the app receives a device token so it can send news and holiday alerts. The token does not identify you by name.
  • News and holiday data (Google Firebase Firestore, GitHub Pages): the app downloads news and holiday lists. No personal data is sent with these requests.
  • Admin sign-in (Firebase Authentication): used only by the app's news editors. Regular users never sign in.
  • Google Play in-app review and update: handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell data, show ads, or collect your name, phone number, location or contacts.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything saved on your phone. You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

The app is meant for adult government employees and is not directed at children.
